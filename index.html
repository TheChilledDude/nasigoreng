Nasi Goreng Recipe

A delicious Indonesian fried rice dish!

About the Recipe:

Nasi Goreng is a traditional Indonesian fried rice dish. It is made using cooked rice, vegetables, eggs, and a combination of sauces and spices.

The name Nasi Goreng literally means "fried rice" in Indonesian. It is popular in Indonesia and other parts of Southeast Asia.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of cooked and chilled rice
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons of soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon of kecap minis (sweet soy sauce)
  • 1 teaspoon of sambal oelek (chilli paste)
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • Cucumber and tomato slices for serving
  • Salt and pepper

Recipe for Nasi Goreng:

  1. Prepare your ingredients. Make sure your rice is cooked and preferably chilled before frying.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion, garlic and carrot. Stir-fry for approximately 2 minutes.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan. Crack the eggs into the empty side and scramble them.
  5. Add the cooked rice and stir everything together.
  6. Add the soy sauce, kecap minis and sambal oelek. Mix thoroughly until the rice is evenly coated.
  7. Add the chopped spring onion, salt and pepper. Stir-fry for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with cucumber and tomatoe slices. Enjoy your homemade Nasi Goreng!

Recipe Information:

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 10 minutes

Level of Difficulty: Easy

Number of Servings: 2 people

Tip of the Day:

For the best Nasi Goreng, use rice that has been cooked and refrigerated beforehand. Cold rice is less sticky and produces better fried rice.

Remember: Always prepare your ingredients before you start cooking!
